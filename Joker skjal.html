Joker

The Phantom Thief

Joker is the main character of Persona 5 Royal. After being falsely accused of a crime, he moves to Tokyo and attends Shujin Academy. He discovers the Metaverse, where he gains the power to change people's hearts and fight against corrupt adults.

Leader of the Phantom Thieves

Joker is the leader of the Phantom Thieves of Hearts. He can use multiple Personas, which gives him a lot of different abilities in battle. He helps bring the team together and leads them through Palaces to steal the Treasures of corrupt people and make them confess their crimes.
