Futaba Sakura

The Genius Hacker

Futaba Sakura is a brilliant hacker who is really good with computers and technology. She usually prefers staying at home and can find social situations difficult, but she is also curious, funny, and very intelligent.

Oracle

Futaba joins the Phantom Thieves as their navigator. Instead of fighting directly, she uses her hacking skills to help the team during battles and while exploring Palaces. Her support makes her an important member of the group.
