Goro Akechi

The Detective Prince

Goro Akechi is a famous high school detective known as the Detective Prince. He is smart, charming, and popular with the public. However, there is much more to him than his friendly personality suggests.

Crow

As Crow, Akechi fights using a sword and a ray gun. His Persona, Robin Hood, uses Bless skills, while his other Persona, Loki, uses powerful Curse attacks. He is a skilled fighter with a complicated connection to the Phantom Thieves.
