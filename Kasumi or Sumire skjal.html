Kasumi (sumire) Yoshizawa

The Rising Gymnast

Kasumi Yoshizawa is a talented rhythmic gymnast who attends Shujin Academy. She is polite, cheerful, and hardworking, and she takes her gymnastics seriously. She wants to improve herself and live up to her potential.

Violet

As Violet, Sumire fights using a rapier and a gun. Her Persona, Cendrillon, specializes in Bless skills and powerful physical attacks. Her agility and gymnastics skills make her a fast and graceful fighter.
