Yusuke Kitagawa

The Passionate Artist

Yusuke Kitagawa is an art student who studies under the famous painter Ichiryusai Madarame. He is talented and deeply passionate about art, often noticing beauty in things that other people overlook. However, he eventually discovers that the person he admired has been exploiting his students and stealing their work. This makes Yusuke question what he has been taught and what it really means to be an artist.

Fox

Yusuke joins the Phantom Thieves under the codename Fox. After learning the truth about Madarame, he decides to use his abilities to fight against injustice. His Persona, Goemon, gives him powerful ice abilities, and he fights using a katana and a gun. Although he can be strange and overly serious about art, Yusuke genuinely cares about his friends and wants to create something meaningful through his work.
