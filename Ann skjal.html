Ann Takamaki

The Model Student

Ann Takamaki is a student at Shujin Academy who is often judged because of her appearance and modeling career. Despite the way others treat her, she is kind, caring, and willing to stand up for people who are being mistreated. She can be playful and cheerful, but she also has a strong sense of justice and refuses to let others control her.

Panther

Ann joins the Phantom Thieves under the codename Panther. After awakening to her Persona, Carmen, she decides to fight against people who abuse their power. In battle, she uses a whip and a gun, while her strong fire abilities make her dangerous against enemies weak to fire. She also uses her charm and determination to support her friends and help the team change people's hearts.
