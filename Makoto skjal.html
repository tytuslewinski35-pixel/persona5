Makoto Niijima

The Student Council Prezident

Makoto Niijima is the student council president at Shujin Academy. At first, she tries to follow the rules and meet everyone's expectations, but she eventually learns to stand up for herself and fight for what's right.

Queen

As Queen, Makoto uses powerful physical attacks and nuclear skills. Her Persona, Johanna, reflects her strong sense of justice and determination to protect others.
