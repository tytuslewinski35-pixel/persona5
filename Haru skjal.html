Haru Okumura

The Gentle Heiress

Haru Okumura is the daughter of the CEO of Okumura Foods. She is kind, polite, and loves gardening. Even though she comes from a wealthy family, she wants to make her own choices and help people who are being treated unfairly.

Noir

As Noir, Haru fights using an axe and a grenade launcher. Her Persona, Milady, uses psy skills and powerful attacks. She is a strong fighter who can deal heavy damage and support her teammates.
