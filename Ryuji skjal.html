Ryuji Sakamoto

The Troublemaker

Ryuji Sakamoto is a student at Shujin Academy and one of the first people to join the Phantom Thieves. He has a reputation as a troublemaker because of his attitude and past conflicts with the school's track team. Even though he can be loud and impulsive, he is a loyal friend who cares deeply about others.

Skull

Ryuji joins the Phantom Thieves under the codename Skull. He fights on the front lines using heavy weapons and powerful physical attacks. His Persona, Captain Kidd, gives him strong electric abilities, making him useful against enemies weak to electricity. Despite his rough personality, Ryuji is one of the most loyal members of the team.
