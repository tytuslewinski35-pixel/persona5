Morgana

The Mysterious Cat

Morgana is a mysterious creature who meets Joker in the Metaverse. Even though he looks like a cat in the real world, he insists that he is not a cat. He has a lot of knowledge about Palaces and the Metaverse, but he doesn't remember everything about his past. He is confident, curious, and determined to discover who he really is.

Mona

Morgana becomes an important member of the Phantom Thieves and helps guide them through the Metaverse. His codename is Mona, and he can transform into a bus to help the team travel through Mementos. He also fights using a slingshot and a curved sword, while his Persona, Zorro, gives him powerful wind abilities and healing skills. Deep down, Morgana hopes to become human one day.
