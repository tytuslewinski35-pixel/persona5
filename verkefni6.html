Persona 5 Royal

Persona 5 Royal is a Japanese role-playing game developed by Atlus. You play as a high school student who moves to Tokyo and starts living a double life. During the day, you go to school and spend time with friends. In another world, you become a Phantom Thief and explore mysterious Palaces.

The Story

The main story follows the Phantom Thieves as they try to change the hearts of people who abuse their power. They enter Palaces, which are worlds created from a person's distorted desires.

Gameplay

Persona 5 Royal combines turn-based combat with everyday life. You can go to school, make friends, improve your abilities and explore Tokyo. During battles, you use Personas with different abilities and weaknesses.
